# B4. Rate: the engine against a Stockfish ladder

A rating for the engine as it stands, so that every later change can be measured against it.

## What it is

- `tools/ladder.sh`: runs the engine against Stockfish 19 at a list of `UCI_Elo` levels with fastchess and writes one PGN per level under `matches/<run>/`.
- `tools/rate.py`: reads those PGNs and fits one rating for the engine, with a 95% interval, and counts illegal moves, crashes and time losses by the engine.
- `.tools/` (not tracked): fastchess 1.8.2-alpha (mac arm64), Stockfish 19 (macOS universal) and the `noob_3moves.epd` opening set, all fetched from their GitHub releases; the sha256 of each is on the ledger with the prediction.

## Method

- Opponent: Stockfish 19, `Threads 1`, `Hash 16`, `UCI_LimitStrength true`, `UCI_Elo` set to each level. Stockfish documents `UCI_Elo` (1320 to 3190) as calibrated at 120 s + 1 s and anchored to CCRL 40/4, so the rating below is on that scale and says nothing about Lichess ratings.
- Engine: `target/release/uci`, default graph, one thread, no hash table.
- Games: 10 s + 0.1 s for both, openings from `noob_3moves.epd` in random order with a fixed seed (`-srand 20261019`), each opening played twice with colors reversed, resignation at 800 centipawns for four moves, draw adjudication from move 60 at 5 centipawns for ten moves.
- Sweep: 20 openings (40 games) at each of 1320, 1500, 1700, 1900, 2100 and 2300. Refinement: 100 openings (200 games) at each of the three levels nearest the sweep's even-score point. Total at least 840 games.
- Fit: one rating R for the engine, solving that the games' scores sum to the sum of the expected scores 1 / (1 + 10^((L - R) / 400)) over all games at all levels. The interval comes from the variance of the per-game residuals.
- Calibration check: 200 games at 60 s + 0.6 s against the level nearest the fitted rating, compared with the 10 s + 0.1 s fit.

## Predictions for the first run on the Mac

1. No illegal move, crash or time loss by the engine anywhere in the sweep and the refinement.
2. The fitted rating is between 1500 and 2000.
3. The 95% interval is within 50 Elo of the fitted rating on each side, over at least 600 games.
4. The 60 s + 0.6 s check gives a rating within 100 Elo of the 10 s + 0.1 s fit.

## Not in this increment

A transposition table, games against Maia or against engines on the CCRL list, the Lichess bot account (B5).

## Results (first run on the Mac, 19 to 20 October 2026)

Engine commit 8b9bba0, Stockfish 19, fastchess 1.8.2-alpha. The ledger record is `chess.b4.result`.

| Stockfish level | Time | Games | Engine score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1320 | 10 s + 0.1 s | 40 | 92.5% |
| 1500 | 10 s + 0.1 s | 40 | 87.5% |
| 1700 | 10 s + 0.1 s | 240 | 61.0% |
| 1900 | 10 s + 0.1 s | 240 | 46.5% |
| 2100 | 10 s + 0.1 s | 240 | 30.4% |
| 2300 | 10 s + 0.1 s | 40 | 17.5% |
| 1900 | 60 s + 0.6 s | 200 | 56.5% |

- **Baseline: 1868 ± 31 (95%)** over the 840 ten-second games, on Stockfish's `UCI_Elo` scale. The 600-game refinement alone gives 1858 ± 35.
- **Longer time control:** 1945 ± 49 over 200 games at 60 s + 0.6 s against level 1900, 77 above the baseline and about 1.3 standard errors from it.
- **Protocol:** no illegal move, crash or time loss by the engine in any of the 1,040 games.

1. No illegal move, crash or time loss. **Held.**
2. A fitted rating between 1500 and 2000. **Held:** 1868.
3. A 95% interval within 50 Elo on each side over at least 600 games. **Held:** ±31 over 840.
4. The 60 s + 0.6 s rating within 100 of the ten-second fit. **Held:** 77 apart.

Caveats. The scores fall off more slowly between 1900 and 2300 than a logistic curve through the lower levels would give, so the fit depends on which levels are included. Stockfish documents `UCI_Elo` as calibrated at 120 s + 1 s; the 60 s check tests the time control only partly. The rating is on Stockfish's scale and is not a Lichess rating.

Every later change to the engine is measured with `tools/ladder.sh` and `tools/rate.py` against this baseline, at 10 s + 0.1 s against levels 1700, 1900 and 2100.
