# B14. Plain speech: no tags, "you" for the student and "I" for Symbia

Brian read the three lines in a real game and said they were much better, then asked for them to sound natural: instead of a label such as "You:", the line should open the way a person would, with "you moved" or "you have", and Symbia's own lines should say "I". This increment removes the three tags and rewrites the wording so that each line carries its voice in the first words.

## What changes

**The tags go.** The coach no longer puts "Game: ", "You: " or "Me: " in front of a line. The three lanes stay in the coach as they are, with their own events, rules for dropping a stale line, and order of arrival; only the text changes. Every line now has the full 140 characters, six more than before for the You and Game lines and a few more for Me.

**The Game lane is unchanged.** It already read "You moved your pawn to h6. It attacks my bishop on g4, which is loose." That sentence needs no label.

**The You lane opens with "You".**

| Case | Now | Before |
|---|---|---|
| the move I would play | You played the move I would play. | That is the move I would play. |
| nearly as good | You played nearly as well as my choice, knight to f3. | About as good as my choice, knight to f3. |
| Good | You played a good move. I like knight to f3 a little more. | Good move. I like knight to f3 a little more. |
| Inaccuracy, Mistake, Blunder | You made a mistake that costs about 2 pawns. I can take your queen on d4. I would play knight to f3. | Mistake: it costs about 2 pawns. I can take your queen on d4. Knight to f3 was better. |
| a mate allowed | You allowed mate in 2. I would play knight to f3. | Blunder: that allows mate in 2. Knight to f3 was better. |
| a mate missed | You missed mate in 2 with queen to h5. | Blunder: you had mate in 2 with queen to h5. |

The ending "Knight to f3 was better." became "I would play knight to f3." (and "I would castle kingside." for castling), so the line stays in Symbia's voice. Shorter forms drop the last clause first, as before. The classes, the costs and the search are not touched. A line about a mate names no class, so the replay counts those separately.

**The Me lane opens with "I".** The first clause of `narrate::say` said what the move did without a subject ("Taking your pawn on d4."). It now says it with one.

| Case | Now | Before |
|---|---|---|
| a capture | I took your pawn on d4. | Taking your pawn on d4. |
| a threat | I am attacking your knight on c6, which is loose. | Attacking your knight on c6, which is loose. |
| a loose piece moved | I moved my loose bishop out of danger. | Moving my loose bishop out of danger. |
| a piece defended | I defended my bishop on g5. | Defending my bishop on g5. |
| a check | I gave check. | Giving check. |
| castling | I castled kingside. | Castling. |
| a promotion | I promoted to a queen. | Promoting to a queen. |
| nothing to say | I made a quiet move. | A quiet move. |

The clauses after the first ("Your bishop on e3 is loose.", "My bishop on g5 is loose.", "I am slightly better.", "I looked 3 moves ahead.") already used the same voice and are unchanged.

**The introduction.** The bot's two opening lines described the tags. They are rewritten to say what the three lines are without naming them.

## What stays the same

The search, the evaluation, the engine's moves and node counts. The first seven columns of the reference fingerprint are expected to be byte-equal to B13's; the eighth, the sentence, changes by design. The judge's classes and costs. The outbox rules.

## Found along the way

**"About 1 pawns."** B13 said "it costs about 1 pawns" for a Mistake that cost 125 to 149 centipawns, and the line is live on the hosted bot. My B13 checks tested what the lines said about the board, not their grammar, so the replay never caught it; reading the new wording is what showed it. The cost clause now says "about a pawn" for any cost that rounds to one, and a test runs every cost from 0 to 2,499.

**The ending.** "Knight to f3 was better." became "I would play knight to f3." (and "I would castle kingside."). It keeps the line in Symbia's voice, and it removes the move name at the front of the sentence, which read badly for captures ("Knight takes pawn on f2 was better").

**A timing test.** The unit test that the ponder stops promptly had a limit of 25 ms, which is a release-build figure (0.78 ms in B13). Run in the debug build it took 9 ms alone and 33 ms beside the rest of the suite, and failed. The limit is now 100 ms, which still separates a ponder that stops from one that runs for seconds; the release measurement stands.

**The push script died early.** The Mac job that waits for the game to end was stopped by the exec tool's default limit of 120 seconds, before its own clean-up ran, so the private key stayed in a temporary folder on the Mac until I deleted it by hand. The ssh session ended with it, and the staged package does not depend on that session. The next push script should run under a longer limit and remove its files in a separate step.

The package builds as release `47e185467bc4`.
